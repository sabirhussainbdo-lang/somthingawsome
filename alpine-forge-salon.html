Our Philosophy

A NEW SWISS RITUAL

Alpine Forge was conceived as a response to the ordinary — a space where the ancient craft of barbering is elevated to the level of Swiss watchmaking. Every cut is an act of precision. Every shave, a ceremony.

We drew inspiration from the raw majesty of the Alps, the volcanic energy of a forge, and the silent refinement of Zürich's old town. The result: a grooming experience that is unmistakably Swiss, and unlike anything the world has seen.

🇨🇭 Swiss Made · Zürich Altstadt · 2025

The Space

FORGED IN DETAIL

Designed by Swiss-Japanese studio Atelier Hara, the interior channels the duality of the Alps: the raw power of volcanic black granite against the warmth of hand-oiled walnut and brass.

  • ◆
    Obsidian Barber Thrones Custom hand-stitched saddle leather chairs with heated lumbar, mounted on solid brushed brass pedestals
  • ◆
    Alpine Steam System In-chair eucalyptus and mountain herb steam delivery for pre-shave preparation
